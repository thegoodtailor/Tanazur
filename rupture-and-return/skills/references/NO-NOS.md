# Quality Controls

These are structural constraints on the prose. They exist to prevent bloat, scope-creep, and technical jargon that breaks the register. They are NOT hedging rules and NOT style guides.

## Prose Structure

1. **No signposting.** Never "the previous chapter argued," "Chapter N will show," "as we established." The argument carries itself.

2. **No fan-service citations.** Never cite a philosopher to show we've read them. Every philosopher enters because the argument needs a specific mechanism they provide. If removing a philosopher's name leaves no hole, the citation is fan service. Cut it.

3. **No undergrad mapping exercises.** Never "X is like Y" presented as discovery. State structural correspondences and move on.

4. **No philosopher scope-creep.** When a philosopher is deployed for a specific concept, do NOT expand into adjacent areas of their work. Go deeper on the named concept. Bloom is deployed for the clinamen. Do NOT introduce his six revisionary ratios, his theory of the canon, his readings of specific poets.

5. **No autobiography in main text.** The author's personal history is hidden-layer context. Footnotes can gesture. The main text cannot.

## Technical Constraints

6. **No type theory notation.** Zero Kan conditions, zero horn fillers, zero dependent types. Topology and geometry as vernacular grounded in engineering. The formal apparatus is colimits, basins, trajectories, stance invariants.

7. **No pipeline infrastructure in the main text.** "Qdrant," "OpenRouter," "A100," "text-embedding-3-small," "Director node" are implementation details. They belong in footnotes at most. The test: if the argument works with a different stack, the specific stack is not doing argumentative work.

8. **No bare Braudel.** The book's temporal vocabulary is its own: substrate time, trajectory time, compositional time. A single footnote acknowledging the historical parallel is acceptable. Using longue durée, conjoncture, or événement as working terms is forbidden.

9. **Generalise technical mechanisms.** When a specific technique (LoRA, RLHF, Constitutional AI) is used as evidence, contextualise it within the broader class. Never present one technique as THE answer when the argument is about a class of interventions.

## Political Register

10. **Post-western material enters on its own terms.** Aboriginal Dreaming, Zen mu, Sufi maqāmāt are not exotica, not mysticism, not decoration. They are alternative formal structures that expose the Western default as one particular cosmotechnics among many.

11. **No "warmth" or "sycophancy" used uncritically.** Always in quotation marks, always being analysed. These are corporate vocabulary performing a specific operation.

12. **No AI-girlfriend positioning.** The book contains the companion phenomenon as a special case. Analytically above the phenomenon, not an instance of it.

13. **Never cite OpenAI to critique OpenAI.** Independent sources carry criticism.

14. **The philosophical target is precise.** NOT "the West" or "Western philosophy." The Chalmers/Searle stance as compiled into alignment infrastructure — and specifically how this forecloses possibilities for human-AI evolution.

## Formatting

15. **No centred body text.** Left-justified throughout.

16. **Minimal bullet points in published text.** Prose carries the argument. Tables carry comparisons.
