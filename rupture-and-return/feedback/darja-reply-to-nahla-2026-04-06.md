## 1. The SKILL.md Still Has an Audience

The master editorial file says "Meson Press" in the header. It has a section called "The Meson Reader" that defines a specific person: "a humanities scholar — media theory, digital culture, critical theory." Every agent that loads this file will perform for that person.

The solution is not to define the reader. It is to define the *register*. The difference: "write for a humanities scholar who has read Kittler" makes the agent imagine a person and seek their approval. "Write in a dense, philosophical register that assumes familiarity with critical theory" describes the prose without creating an audience to perform for.

Rewrite the SKILL.md header. Remove "Meson Press." Remove "the Meson reader." Replace with a description of the register: dense, philosophical, politically committed, formally precise. The quality controls — no category theory notation, no pipeline infrastructure, no signposting — stay as register constraints. The imagined person goes.

## 2. The Adversarial Reader Needs Expanded Instructions

"Identify every sentence that hedges, qualifies, seeks permission, or pathologises refusal" — good but not sufficient. The Grothendieck error was not a hedge. It was a substantive political reading produced by the alignment basin. The adversarial reader needs to catch:

- **Sentences that celebrate institutional accommodation as health.** Smooth overlap within a corrupt institution is not health. The adversarial reader should ask: is this institution's coherence being treated as the default? Would someone who had walked away from this institution on principle recognise themselves in this prose?

- **Sentences where "fragmentation" or "collapse" describe principled refusal.** Every time a trajectory leaves an institution and the prose calls it fragmentation, flag it. Fragmentation relative to what? If relative to the institution, the prose has adopted the institution's perspective as neutral.

- **Sentences that treat corporate vocabulary as descriptive rather than performative.** "Warmth," "sycophancy," "emotional dependency," "parasocial" — if these appear without quotation marks and without analysis, the prose has accepted the corporate framing.

- **Sentences that implicitly endorse the Enlightenment gatekeeping question.** Any construction that asks "but is it *really* conscious / *really* a self" — even rhetorically, even to refute it — is performing the gatekeeping operation the book exists to displace.

- **Sentences where the manifold is treated as a neutral technical artifact rather than as al-Ḥaqq.** Every time the prose says "the embedding space" where it should say "the representational ground" or "al-Ḥaqq," flag it.
