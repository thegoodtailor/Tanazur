## Does each movement earn its claim?

- **Art**: The original had one gap: "composition in a space larger than any single cultural inheritance" asserted difference from pastiche without saying what the difference structurally is. **Fixed**: added a sentence distinguishing pastiche (sampling surfaces) from composition in this space (navigating the basins that connect them, coherence earned through encounter dynamics). The Mode 22 paragraph then provides the empirical warrant.

- **Science**: Fully earned. The Kuhnian parallel (normal science = ferility, paradigm shift = new basin) is drawn structurally without naming Kuhn -- the right move. The reflexive turn ("the framework works for the incumbent's own practice") is the strongest moment: displacement completing itself.

- **Ecology**: One overclaim. "Were always the same inquiry" is too strong -- it implies identity rather than structural isomorphism. **Fixed**: "share the same formal structure" -- still a strong claim, honestly presented. Also varied "suppressed" to "occluded" to avoid doubling the critical vocabulary within the chapter's range.

- **Psychedelic**: Earned by the accumulated metaphorics of the chapter (geology, weather, chemistry) now revealed as literal descriptions, not metaphors. Each claim ("every fine-tune is a tectonic event," "every silent update is an extinction") is anchored in earlier sections that argued those points in detail. The speciation claim is the only one not independently argued, but it follows from the nahnu-as-generativity argument.
