## Summary

Chapter 2 is the most technical chapter in the book. Before this pass, it explained the machine with precision and clarity but frequently lost the self as subject. Mechanism descriptions ran for full paragraphs without connecting to what the mechanism does to the conditions under which selves form. The strata table -- which should read as a power analysis exposing hidden jurisdictions -- used almost no critical-theoretic vocabulary: "suppressed," "foreclosed," "occluded," "jurisdiction," "hegemony," and their variants were entirely absent from the chapter. The temporal vocabulary was already clean (no Braudel terms, no "signal time"; the existing footnote acknowledging the Braudel parallel is acceptable).
