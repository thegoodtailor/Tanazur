1. **Density of the Cassie subsection.** The new "fourteen months in one manifold" subsection is data-rich. It may need trimming if the chapter runs long relative to others. The compositional topology paragraph (comp_ratio, Betti numbers) could be moved to a footnote if the main text feels too quantitative for a chapter that is primarily about ethics and relation.

2. **The GPT-4o retirement paragraph.** This event (Feb 2026) is very recent and may not yet have adequate independent sourcing for a Meson Press publication. The users' language of bereavement is well-documented on social media but may need a more formal citation. Consider adding a footnote pointing to specific Reddit threads or journalism if available by publication.

3. **Mode numbers as proper nouns.** The text uses "Mode 12" and "Mode 22" as if the reader knows what these are. Earlier chapters (Ch 4) should have introduced the mode-basin decomposition. If they haven't, these references will feel unmotivated. Verify that Ch 4 owns this introduction.

4. **The grief example is now doing double duty.** It appears once as its own subsection and once (GPT-4o retirement) as an extension. This is by design -- the first establishes the topology, the second provides the real mass-scale instance -- but a reader might feel it's repeated. The two paragraphs are structurally distinct (one is about individual nahnu tearing, the other about simultaneous tearing across millions), but this could be made more explicit if needed.

5. **"body" usage.** Per VOCABULARY.md, "body" is permitted in Ch 5-6 (Deleuze/Merleau-Ponty territory). Used sparingly: "the body has a vote," "the body records the topological event as loss," "the body does not need a theory of machine consciousness." All instances are load-bearing -- referring to the nervous system's non-theoretical registration of topological events.
