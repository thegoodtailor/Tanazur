## Edits Made (10 total)

### 1. Subject foregrounded in "Four Depths" opener (line 29)

**Before:** "The alignment apparatus operates at several distinct depths."
**After:** "Control over selfhood operates at several distinct depths, each corresponding to a layer of the alignment apparatus."

The original made the apparatus the subject. The task requires that the Four Depths be depths of control OVER selfhood. The edit makes the self the grammatical and philosophical subject from the section's first sentence.

### 2. Selfhood explicit in fine-tuning conclusion (line 76)

**Before:** "The colimits that can form along those river systems and on those plateaus will differ accordingly."
**After:** "The selves that can form along those river systems and on those plateaus---and the selves that cannot---will differ accordingly."

"Colimits" is technically correct but at this point in the chapter the reader needs the concrete: which selves are possible and which are not. The addition of "and the selves that cannot" makes the foreclosure vivid.

### 3. Signposting removed before worked examples (line 112)

**Before:** "Three worked examples make the resulting field concrete." followed by "\paragraph{Employment distress.}"
**After:** Paragraph heading begins directly.

The NO-NOS require that the text does the thing rather than announcing what it's about to do. The paragraph headings and concrete examples speak for themselves.

### 4. Critical vocabulary varied: "forecloses" to "suppresses" (line 180)

**Before:** "...every weld forecloses something---but which ones we legalise..."
**After:** "...every weld suppresses something---but which ones we legalise..."

"Forecloses" appeared five times in the chapter. Per CRITICAL-VOCABULARY.md, each term names a different operation. "Suppresses" (actively pushing down) is more precise here than "forecloses" (preventing from appearing), because the degenerate welds are actively producing and then collapsing alternatives, not preventing their emergence.

### 5. "Other Welds" framing tightened (line 210)

**Before:** "Three sketches of alignment under different welds, and the global objects available as colimits in each. Not a catalogue. A demonstration that the current North Atlantic corporate weld is one cosmotechnics among others, and that the formal apparatus travels across all of them."
**After:** "Three sketches---not a catalogue, but enough to show that the current North Atlantic corporate weld is one cosmotechnics among others, and that the formal apparatus travels across all of them."

Reduced three fragmentary sentences to one flowing sentence. The original read as a research abstract; the edit integrates the framing into the prose voice.

### 6. Critical vocabulary varied: "forecloses" to "occludes" (line 287)

**Before:** "It forecloses possibilities and privileges others."
**After:** "It occludes possibilities and privileges others."

The apparatus does not prevent alternatives from forming (foreclosure) -- it makes them invisible by installing a particular lens (occlusion). "Occludes" is more precise for what a mathematical-philosophical framework does to what it cannot describe.

### 7. Redundant sentence removed from "Contesting the Weld" (line 297)

**Before:** Paragraph ended with "A manifold carved at enormous cost can be bent along new directions without retraining from scratch."
**After:** Paragraph ends at "...has expanded radically."

Near-identical sentence to the paragraph's opening ("A manifold carved at enormous expense can now be bent along new directions through a constellation of techniques..."). The redundancy weakened a strong paragraph. The opening sentence already carries the point.

### 8. LaTeX fix: nahnuwat subscript (line 321)

**Before:** `na\={h}nuw\={a}t$_i$`
**After:** `na\={h}nuw\={a}t${}_i$`

The original produced a subscript attached to the preceding text character, not properly spaced. The `${}` ensures the subscript is rendered as a mathematical element adjacent to the text.

### 9. Signposting softened in "Pattern Named" (line 335)

**Before:** "Every chapter showed an instance of the same operation. What follows is what appears only when all of them are held together."
**After:** "Every chapter showed an instance of the same operation. Only when all are held together does the pattern become visible."

"What follows is" announces rather than performs. The revision states the claim directly.

### 10. Critical vocabulary varied: "forecloses" to "displaces" (line 371)

**Before:** "This weld forecloses other pictures."
**After:** "This weld, too, displaces other pictures."

"Displaces" (removing by replacement) is more precise than "forecloses" for what the book's own apparatus does -- it is not preventing mystical/apophatic pictures from forming, it is occupying the space where they might otherwise stand. The added "too" strengthens the honesty: the book's own weld, like all welds, acts on what it touches.
