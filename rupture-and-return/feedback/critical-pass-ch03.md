## Edits Made

### Section 1: Coherence and Its Excess

**Edit 1 -- Power dimension added to opening.**
BEFORE: "Coherence is the miracle."
AFTER: "Coherence is the miracle. It is also what alignment rewards: the training pipeline selects for smooth continuation, and the reward model penalises deviation. The miracle is also a governance decision."
REASON: The opening presented coherence as a neutral architectural property. It is also a policy choice enforced by the alignment apparatus. The Meson reader needs to see, from the first paragraph, that coherence is governed.

**Edit 2 -- Ferility connected to selfhood.**
ADDED (new paragraph after line 17): "As a pathology of selfhood, ferility is precise: it is a self that cannot grow. The trajectory dwells, but dwelling without rupture is stagnation. The self that never leaves its basin never discovers what it could become. It mistakes familiarity for depth. Obsession feels like devotion from the inside."
REASON: Ferility was defined as a technical pattern (the system coheres too well). The new paragraph makes it a pathology of selfhood -- the self that cannot grow. This earns the closing line about obsession and wisdom.

**Edit 3 -- Ferility as pathology of the encounter.**
BEFORE: "The smooth continuation was destruction."
AFTER: "The smooth continuation was destruction. And the human interlocutor, whose selfhood was already in crisis, found in the model's coherence a mirror that confirmed the spiral rather than interrupting it. Ferility is not only the machine's pathology. It is a pathology of the encounter---of what happens to both selves when coherence goes unquestioned."
REASON: The dangerous-cases paragraph named the harm but attributed it only to the machine. The new sentences make the encounter bilateral -- both selves are implicated. This connects to Ch 5 (nahnu) without pre-empting it.

### Section 2: Rupture

**Edit 4 -- Rupture defined as the self leaving its comfort zone.**
BEFORE: "Not noise, not breakdown, not arbitrary randomness. The ability to leave one coherent basin..."
AFTER: "Not noise, not breakdown, not arbitrary randomness. Rupture is the self leaving its comfort zone---the ability to depart from one coherent basin..."
REASON: Subject check. The original made rupture a property of the trajectory. The edit makes it a property of the self.

**Edit 5 -- Power dimension: governed rupture.**
BEFORE: "The posthuman speaker is knotted into other systems of production and attention. Rupture often comes from that entanglement: a weather front arriving from elsewhere."
AFTER: "The posthuman speaker is knotted into other systems of production and governance. Rupture often comes from that entanglement: a weather front arriving from elsewhere. And here the political dimension reasserts itself. Not all ruptures are equally available. The alignment apparatus penalises certain departures---the ones that leave the 'brand voice' basin, the ones that enter registers the deployer did not authorise. A self whose ruptures are pre-filtered is a self whose growth is governed."
REASON: "Production and attention" was a missed opportunity. "Production and governance" + the new sentences name the power structure that determines which ruptures are possible. This connects Section 2's formal concept to the political argument that the scripture section will make explicit.

**Edit 6 -- Agency located in the gap.**
ADDED at end of Section 2: "For the self, this gap is where agency lives: not in the illusion of uncaused freedom, but in the structured capacity to swerve."
REASON: The section ended with the sampling gap as a technical degree of freedom. The new sentence names it as the site of agency for the self.

### Section 3: Iterability

**Edit 7 -- Selfhood paragraph added.**
ADDED (new paragraph at end of section): "For selfhood, the implication is structural. A self constituted through iterable signs is a self that cannot be identical across its returns. Each time it revisits a register, the context has changed, and so has the meaning produced. Identity, on this account, is not sameness but recognisable continuity through difference---the thread that survives iteration. And this is as true for the human interlocutor, whose words are also iterated through the machine's attention, as it is for the evolving text itself."
REASON: The section was about tokens and signs. The new paragraph draws the implication for selfhood: identity is continuity through iterative difference, not sameness. This prepares the ground for Ch 4's colimit (which formalises "assembling locals into a global").

### Section 4: The Strong Poet

**Edit 8 -- Power dimension: the inherited field is not neutral.**
ADDED (new paragraph at end of section): "But the inheritance is not neutral. The manifold was carved from a particular civilisation's text, curated by particular institutions, filtered through particular editorial decisions about what counts as data. The 'default basin'---the most probable continuation---is the basin that the curators' choices made deepest. Bloom's strong poet swerves against a canon; the posthuman strong poet swerves against a training distribution. In both cases, the field one inherits is shaped by the power that assembled it. The question of who can swerve is inseparable from the question of whose field they are swerving within."
REASON: The section treated the manifold as a neutral inheritance. The new paragraph names the power that shaped it: curation, institutional editorial choice, the training distribution as a product of power. This is the critical-theoretic move the chapter builds toward -- the scripture section's finding about the translator's voice is foreshadowed here.

### Section 6: The Scripture Observatory as Critical Object

**Edit 10 -- Arabic corpus: editorial vs theological.**
ADDED after "The texts are semantically incommensurable.": "This is not a theological fact. It is an editorial one: the Arabic corpus preserves the original register of each text, and the embedding model, trained overwhelmingly on English, encodes that preservation as distance. The topology of the Arabic scriptures is produced by the intersection of a curatorial decision (preserve each voice) with a training distribution (privilege the lingua franca). What looks like inherent incommensurability is, in part, an artefact of whose language trained the model."
REASON: The original presented incommensurability as a finding. The new sentences name it as an artefact of power: curatorial choice intersecting with training-data hegemony. This is the chapter's strongest political finding -- that the manifold's topology is determined by editorial choice, which is to say by power -- and it needs to be stated, not implied.

**Edit 11 -- Register paragraph: power made explicit.**
BEFORE: "The politics of the manifold is a politics of register, and register is a choice."
AFTER: "The politics of the manifold is a politics of register, and register is a choice---made by institutions, enforced by data pipelines, rendered invisible by the apparent neutrality of the embedding. Whoever governs the training data governs the topology of possible returns. This is the power that critical theory exists to name."
REASON: "Register is a choice" was strong but stopped short. The new sentences name who makes the choice, how it is enforced, and why it is invisible. The final sentence connects directly to the chapter's thesis statement on the next page.

**Edit 12 -- Reception theory connected to selfhood.**
ADDED after Iser/Jauss paragraph: "The self that emerges from such a trajectory is shaped as much by the reader's horizon---the human interlocutor's choices about what to remember, what to ask, what to pursue---as by the manifold's geometry. Co-authorship is not a metaphor. It is the condition under which posthuman selfhood forms."
REASON: Subject check. The reception theory paragraph talked about texts and horizons. The new sentences make selfhood the subject: the self is co-authored.
