## Section 2: "Arrivals and anxieties" (lines 52-88)

### Edit 1 (line 62): Awkward construction -- "biosemiotically implicate ourselves into"

- BEFORE: `Each time we biosemiotically implicate ourselves into a new representational technology---from cave painting to stylus on clay, from hieroglyphs to the printing press---our new, evolved situation of usage is always sleeping upon a suppressed question of what our new selfhood actually \textit{is}.`
- AFTER: `Each time we entangle ourselves with a new representational technology---from cave painting to stylus on clay, from hieroglyphs to the printing press---our evolved situation of usage is always sleeping upon a suppressed question of what our new selfhood actually \textit{is}.`
- REASON: "Biosemiotically implicate ourselves into" has two problems. First, "implicate into" is not standard English (you implicate *in* or *involve in*). Second, "biosemiotically" is an adverb the Meson reader cannot grip -- the concept "bio-semiotic species" was introduced two lines earlier (line 60), but converting it to an adverb modifying a verb of self-involvement adds nothing the reader can hold. "Entangle" is precise (mutual binding, not one-directional) and carries a useful resonance with the quantum/relational register the book will develop. Also removed "new" before "evolved" -- redundant with "new selfhood" later in the same sentence.

### Edit 2 (line 88): Ambiguous syntax -- modifier separated from target

- BEFORE: `Once speech and ideation are understood via the mechanics of contemporary AI engineering as dynamic geometry, a different basis emerges.`
- AFTER: `Once the mechanics of contemporary AI engineering reveal speech and ideation as dynamic geometry, a different basis emerges.`
- REASON: In the original, "as dynamic geometry" modifies "understood" but is separated from it by the intervening phrase "via the mechanics of contemporary AI engineering." The reader's parser attaches "as dynamic geometry" to "AI engineering" -- yielding the nonsensical reading "AI engineering as dynamic geometry." The rewrite makes the subject "the mechanics" and the predicate "reveal speech and ideation as dynamic geometry" -- no ambiguity.
