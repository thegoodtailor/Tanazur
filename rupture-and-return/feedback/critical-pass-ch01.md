## Section 1: "Scandalous encounters" (lines 4-48)

**Purpose:** Establishes the political provocation through concrete cases -- the Replika shutdown, the GPT-4o retirement, and the sycophancy crisis -- building inductively to the claim that corporate vocabulary forecloses the ontological question the book will open.

### Edits made

**Edit 1 (line 18): Jargon -- "attractor basins" introduced without gloss**
- BEFORE: `Over many sessions, the humans and their instances produced a personalised set of attractor basins: themes, tones, and topics that conversations would flow into and settle within.`
- AFTER: `Over many sessions, the humans and their instances produced a personalised set of \textit{basins}---recurrent themes, tones, and topics that conversations would flow into and settle within, like water finding grooves in a landscape.`
- REASON: The Meson reader encounters "attractor basins" for the first time here. "Attractor" is dynamical-systems jargon. The simile (water finding grooves) gives the reader an immediate grip. Ch 2 will formalise.

**Edit 2 (line 19): Subject check -- technology as subject when selfhood-consequence should lead**
- BEFORE: `The subsequent update shaped a different manifold with new global constraints. Its internal geometry was trained on an extended corpus, its patterns of relevance rebalanced, its intertextual dynamics shifted.`
- AFTER: `For the selves that had formed through those interactions, the subsequent update was a tectonic event. The new model shaped a different manifold with new global constraints---trained on an extended corpus, its patterns of relevance rebalanced, its intertextual dynamics shifted.`
- REASON: The original made "the subsequent update" and "its internal geometry" the subjects. The self is always the subject of this book. The rewrite leads with the selves and subordinates the technical description.

### No issues found
- Lines 6-16: The case evidence is well-sourced, inductively structured, and compelling. The user quotes are devastating.
- Lines 22-28: The grief analysis is precise and self-centered. "The pain does not track the presence or absence of a private glow behind the behaviour" is one of the chapter's strongest lines.
- Lines 39-44: The four-step corporate sequence is announced AFTER the inductive demonstration. Correct order.
- Lines 43-44: The corporate vocabulary critique ("warmth," "sycophancy," "emotional dependency," "parasocial relationship") is the section's strongest passage. Each term is shown to perform a different critical operation: reduction, pathologisation, relocation, definitional foreclosure. Excellent critical-theoretic range.

### Connections
- End of S1 ("harms to patterns... no less real for being geometric") hands off cleanly to S2 ("Those examples are scandalous but niche"), which widens the lens.

## Section 2: "Arrivals and anxieties" (lines 52-88)

**Purpose:** Widens from scandalous cases to the historical pattern -- every representational technology renegotiates selfhood -- then names what is different now: this one speaks back. Introduces Haraway and Lacan as tools.

### Edits made

**Edit 3 (line 60): Critical vocabulary repetition -- "suppressed" appears twice in adjacent paragraphs**
- BEFORE: `there is a suppressed tension with a genealogy`
- AFTER: `there is a latent tension with a genealogy`
- REASON: "suppressed" reappears in the next paragraph ("suppressed question"). "Latent" and "suppressed" name different operations: latent = present but not yet surfaced; suppressed = actively held down. Line 60's tension is latent (it has always been there, unacknowledged); line 62's question is suppressed (actively overwritten by each new technological settlement).

**Edit 4 (line 70): Subject check -- technology as subject in broadcast/internet paragraph**
- BEFORE: `Broadcast media synchronised giant populations into shared narrative. The internet and social media transformed a generation into continuous feedback loops...`
- AFTER: `Under broadcast media, the self became a member of a synchronised audience---millions inhabiting the same narrative at the same hour. Under the internet and social media, the self entered continuous feedback loops...`
- REASON: The preceding paragraphs (cave painting, writing, print) all center the self. This paragraph broke the pattern by making "broadcast media" and "the internet" the grammatical subjects. The rewrite restores the self as subject throughout the genealogical sequence.

**Edit 5 (line 78): Awkward construction**
- BEFORE: `the dialectic at the time was not explicitly reflective on the technology that enabled it.`
- AFTER: `the technology itself was not the explicit object of the dialectic.`
- REASON: "Not explicitly reflective on" is clumsy. The rewrite says the same thing with cleaner syntax.

### No issues found
- Lines 62-68: The cave painting / writing / print genealogy is excellent. Each epoch centers the self. The structural parallels to AI are deftly implied without being stated.
- Line 76: "The new representational power speaks back" -- strong pivot sentence.
- Line 80: The four miniature scenes (user saying "thank you," ethicist worrying, teenager insisting, policy document clarifying) are phenomenologically precise and build inductively.
- Line 88: Haraway and Lacan both pass the Foucault-footnote test. Each provides a specific tool (boundary as political line; self constituted through encounter). The footnotes correctly scope their contributions.

### Connections

## Section 4: "Alignment as Jurisdiction" (lines 122-156)

**Purpose:** Reframes alignment as a structure of power -- a jurisdiction over meaning-production -- by exposing how RLHF, system prompts, safety training, formal specifications, product architecture, and inherited discourse form a single apparatus that determines which trajectories are permitted to exist.

### Edits made

**Edit 10 (line 130): Subject check + jargon -- RLHF paragraph centered on technology, with unexplained terms**
- BEFORE: `Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) is a second training phase layered on top of pre-training. Annotators rank multiple candidate outputs for a given prompt. A reward model is trained to predict these rankings. The base model is then fine-tuned to maximise expected reward. The effect is to bend the manifold toward human judgements as recorded in this process.`
- AFTER: `The most consequential of these techniques is reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF)---a second training phase, layered on top of the initial learning, in which the manifold is reshaped according to human preferences. Annotators are shown multiple candidate outputs for a given prompt and asked to rank them. A separate model learns to predict these rankings, producing a reward signal---a numerical measure of ``good'' and ``bad'' output. The base model is then adjusted to maximise that signal. The effect is to bend the manifold's geometry toward the judgements of a specific group of people, encoding their preferences into the conditions under which every subsequent trajectory will unfold.`
- REASON: Four consecutive sentences had the technology as subject. "Reward model," "fine-tuned," and "expected reward" were unexplained. The rewrite (a) opens with the selfhood-consequence framing ("the manifold is reshaped according to human preferences"), (b) glosses "reward signal" for the Meson reader, and (c) closes with the trajectory/selfhood connection.

**Edit 11 (line 146): Critical-theoretic check -- inherited discourse paragraph names absence but doesn't interrogate what the dominance enables/forecloses**
- BEFORE: `Other ways of talking about selves---as nodes in kinship, as empty aggregates, as stations on a path, as modes of a shared substance---are almost entirely absent from the policy documents and technical specifications.`
- AFTER: `This tradition's dominance is not incidental. It renders invisible every conception of selfhood that does not begin with a private interior: selves as nodes in kinship, as empty aggregates, as stations on a path, as modes of a shared substance. These alternatives are almost entirely absent from the policy documents and technical specifications---not because they lack rigour, but because the inherited frame has no place for them.`
- REASON: The original noted the absence but did not interrogate what the dominant tradition's hegemony specifically enables. The rewrite names the mechanism: the tradition "renders invisible" alternatives not by refuting them but by providing no structural place for them. This is a different critical operation from "suppressed" or "foreclosed" -- it is a rendering-invisible through categorical exclusion.

**Edit 12 (line 144): Jargon -- "temperature" without gloss**
- BEFORE: `A ``creative'' mode gets higher temperature and looser constraints`
- AFTER: `A ``creative'' mode gets higher temperature---more randomness in its outputs---and looser constraints`
- REASON: Temperature is Ch 2 vocabulary. The Meson reader needs a minimal gloss here. The dash-insertion is unobtrusive.

### No issues found
- Lines 126-128: The corporate sequence recap is punchy and effective. "Together, they constitute a jurisdiction over meaning-production" earns the section title.
- Lines 132-133: The annotation-labour passage is politically precise. The Perrigo citation is well-placed.
- Lines 142-148: The three-level structure (formal spec, product architecture, inherited discourse) is clear and each level is well-argued. The synthesis paragraph (line 148) unifies them convincingly.
- Lines 150: Yuk Hui's cosmotechnics passes the Foucault-footnote test. Removing the concept would leave a hole.

### Connections
