## Assessment

Chapter 5 was already the strongest chapter in the book for maintaining the relational self as subject. The main weaknesses were:
1. **Pipeline leakage:** "Mistral LoRA," "Director," "Kitab al-Tanazur" appearing in argumentative prose rather than footnotes. Fixed.
2. **Critical-theoretic intensity:** The companion economy sections named the structural arrangements but did not always name who profits from them, who is dispossessed by them, or how they reproduce themselves. Strengthened throughout with varied vocabulary.
3. **Grief as product of governance, not accident:** The grief cases were described topologically but the causal chain (corporate decision -> destroyed nahnu -> damaged selfhood) was not always made explicit. Sharpened.

The chapter compiles cleanly (pdflatex, 196 pages total, no errors).
