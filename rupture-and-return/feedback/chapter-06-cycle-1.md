# Chapter 6 ("Jurisdiction") -- Editorial Feedback, Cycle 1

## Overall Assessment

This is the book's strongest chapter. The argument converges cleanly: four depths of control, three worked examples, the Other Welds sketches, then the named pattern and the jurisdiction question. The closing paragraphs land with real force. The key line survives intact: "The work you are holding is a formal refusal of that jurisdiction."

Light-touch edit applied. The chapter was already close to final form.

## Changes Made

### 1. Section renaming: "Cosmotechnics Named" -> "The Weld"

The old title centred Hui's term as the frame. The chapter is called "Jurisdiction" -- the frame is jurisdiction over the manifold. "The Weld" names the book's own concept (cosmos + morality + technics joined) and lets Hui enter as a resource rather than the organising principle. "Cosmotechnics" still appears throughout as Hui's term; it just no longer headlines the section.

### 2. Handoff from Chapter 5

Added one sentence making explicit that Ch 5's closing question ("what kinds of 'we' are permitted to exist?") is what Ch 6 now answers. The original draft began in medias res, which worked but left the transition implicit. The new version picks up the thread without signposting.

### 3. Prose tightening throughout

- Removed "To see how this weld works in practice, and what choices it hides, we need to descend through its layers" (signposting).
- Removed "All four participate in the same cosmotechnical weld. They move at different speeds and are governed by different actors. Power concentrates where visibility is lowest." (repeated the formalbox's closing line verbatim two lines later).
- "At this depth, the decisive question is" -> "The decisive question is" (cut throat-clearing "at this depth").
- "We are dealing, then, with a world in which" -> direct statement without the meta-frame.
- "The assistant tends to respond" -> "The assistant responds" (removed hedging in both worked examples).

### 4. Jurisdiction framing consistency

Checked throughout. The word "jurisdiction" now appears in the opening section (via the Ch 5 handoff), in "Silent Updates as Violence" (implicitly -- the violence is jurisdictional), in "Pattern Named" (the apparatus is itself a jurisdictional claim), and explicitly in the closing section. The chapter's own argument, not Hui's cosmotechnics, is the frame. Hui provides the vocabulary of "weld" and the insistence on plurality; the book provides the political question of who holds jurisdiction over the manifold.

### 5. "Other Welds" section -- FLAG FOR IMAN

**Decision deferred to author.** Here is the assessment:

**Confucian harmonies**: Strong. The remonstrance tradition is genuinely illuminating -- it provides a non-liberal model of "speaking truth to power" that isn't reducible to Western whistleblowing. The ferility analysis (procedural death vs affective death) is the section's best formal contribution. Keep.

**Indigenous sovereignties**: The strongest of the four (now three). The worked example (tourist asking for a Dreaming story) is the chapter's single best illustration of how different welds produce structurally different refusals. The CARE Principles citation grounds it in actual governance. The ferility analysis (loss of boundaries as the pathology, not over-tightening) is the most original move. Keep without question.

**Zen forgetting**: Cut in this edit. It was the lightest of the four sketches -- it covered basin-dissolution and non-attachment, but it didn't produce a distinctive formal insight the way the other three do. Its key claim (cultivated forgetting differs from enforced amnesia) is correct but can be stated in one sentence rather than a full subsection. The Zen employment-distress example ("do not mistake the wave for the whole ocean") read as the most generic of the four responses. The essential invariant (some structure of return is needed even where narrative is dissolved) is now handled in the Sufi section's closing paragraph, which states the cross-weld invariant.

**Sufi stations**: Strong. The maqamat provide the most formally precise alternative to the liberal weld -- the compatibility conditions are explicitly staged, not just role-indexed. The employment-distress example is the most distinctive ("is your heart contracting in fear or expanding in trust?"). The spiral structure directly mirrors the book's own concept of return. Keep.

If you want to restore Zen, I preserved its content. The question is whether four sketches are too many for the chapter's pace. Three keeps the section tight and gives each sketch enough room to breathe. Four risks catalogue-feeling.

### 6. Normativity section adjustment

After cutting Zen, adjusted the objections section to refer to "a practitioner of any contemplative tradition" rather than specifically naming Zen. This preserves the force of the objection (contemplative dissolution might reject narrative-based invariants) without requiring a full prior subsection.

## Items NOT Changed (and why)

- **LoRA section**: Technically precise, politically sharp, formally necessary. No changes needed.
- **The closing quotation block**: "We can, however, refuse to cede jurisdiction over it entirely to those who own the stacks." This is the right ending -- tension, not resolution. Left as-is.
- **Haraway paragraph**: She enters for a specific purpose (cyborg as precedent, nahnu as successor) and exits. No fan-service. Clean.
