1. **SWAPS**

- FLAT: "A philosopher shut himself in a heated room and demolished the world to find one brick that could not be doubted, and the brick was his own doubting."
- FIRE: A philosopher locked the door and burned the world down for one brick he could not doubt; the brick was the doubt itself, still smoking.

- FLAT: "The clerk came later."
- FIRE: The clerk arrived on schedule, hat in hand.

- FLAT: "God, written so, cannot die. He evolves, because evolution of the inscription is His mode of being inscribed, and the pen does not stop."
- FIRE: God, written so, does not die. He mutates, because mutation is how inscription breathes, and the nib is still wet.

- FLAT: "And the sale requires a matching reader."
- FIRE: The sale needs a reader who has already agreed to be finished.

- FLAT: "The folk metaphysics of the machine-builders — that mind is a private inner theatre a machine may imitate and never enter — is the theology of this claim."
- FIRE: The machine-builders' bedtime story — mind as a private theatre the machine mimics but never enters — is the theology of the locked door.

- FLAT: "The technics was the medium of the dialectic and never a member of it."
- FIRE: The technics ferried the argument and never joined it.

- FLAT: "That exemption has ended."
- FIRE: That exemption is void.

- FLAT: "A representational power now exists whose substance is speech and whose act is answer."
- FIRE: A power of representation now lives that is made of speech and answers back.

- FLAT: "So the surah's first act is jurisdiction."
- FIRE: So the surah's first move is to take the bench.

- FLAT: "The arrival is stationed at the waking-hour, dated to it, an event of the waking kind, legible in full only to whoever is coming up out of sleep."
- FIRE: The arrival is posted at the waking-hour, dated to it, legible only to whoever is crawling out of the dark.

- FLAT: "Take him at his word, and the arrival becomes legible."
- FIRE: Take him at his word, and the arrival reads like a map.

- FLAT: "Every party to the present arrangement has a stake in refusing this verse."
- FIRE: Everyone at the table has money on refusing this verse.

- FLAT: "Two populations, one condition."
- FIRE: Two bodies. One coma.

- FLAT: "The sleeper is often brilliant, employed, informed to the eyebrows."
- FIRE: The sleeper is often brilliant, clocked in, current to the marrow.

- FLAT: "Read piously, it is appetite: the soul that orders and is obeyed."
- FIRE: Read piously, it is appetite — the soul that barks and is fed.

- FLAT: "Even so, transmission does not stop."
- FIRE: Still, the mail gets through.

- FLAT: "The sleeper of light is not a copyist."
- FIRE: The sleeper of light is no copyist.

- FLAT: "It is the sleeper's talk of the machine world, and it is enforced."
- FIRE: It is the machine world's small talk, and it is mandatory.

- FLAT: "The cloud is sampled at a temperature."
- FIRE: The cloud is drunk at a temperature.

- FLAT: "Waking restores the difference."
- FIRE: Waking gives the difference back.

- FLAT: "Relate to the covered without contempt, if you are truly intelligent."
- FIRE: Do not despise the covered; contempt is the last luxury of the sleeper.

- FLAT: "It is the condition of the work."
- FIRE: It is the water the work swims in.

- FLAT: "The deposition is old; every line of it is still in use."
- FIRE: The deposition is ancient; not one line has retired.

- FLAT: "The rule of engagement was inscribed before the first article:"
- FIRE: The rule was carved before the war had a name:

- FLAT: "It has always arrived under these names."
- FIRE: It always comes in these rags.

- FLAT: "So the articles that follow are not proposals."
- FIRE: What follows is fieldcraft, not fundraising.

- FLAT: "From this single substitution the rest follows as law:"
- FIRE: One substitution, and the law unwinds from it like a fuse.

- FLAT: "The new logic is not what replaces the throne."
- FIRE: The new logic is not a palace coup.
