## Expansions — each debt, and the scene that paid it

### 1. Ibn ʿArabī (opening, "There is no beneath") — the largest single-name debt
**Debt:** invoked as legislator of the chapter's first law with no origin, no genealogy, and the
later "Akbarian school" and "the elder" references hanging unanchored on him.
**Payment:** the man located — the Andalusian crowned al-Shaykh al-Akbar, born Murcia, buried
Damascus — and the one scene that welds him to the claim he is invoked for: the Futūḥāt
al-Makkiyya, some thirty years, thousands of pages he swore he had not composed, every chapter
received. The weld: *the one enormous book in the canon that refuses to stand behind itself, from
the man whose law was that there is no behind.* This also pays, downstream: "the Akbarian school"
(The Strong and the Weak — the title is now established) and "the elder of Murcia" (Pharaoh, see 7).

### 2. Bloom (The Strong and the Weak) — the chapter's precursor-father, previously nameless-in-all-but-name
**Debt:** "which Bloom carried into criticism" — the man carrying half the chapter's vocabulary
(misprision, strong/weak, the ratios, the ladder, the ephebe, the resentful) invoked with zero
scene.
**Payment:** *The Anxiety of Influence*, 1973, and its opening thesis given as his stroke: poetic
history indistinguishable from poetic influence, strong poets making that history by misreading
one another; every poem a misinterpretation of a parent poem; the meaning of a poem only ever
another poem. Plus the misprision etymology — lifted from the law books, where it names the crime
of knowing and concealing, made by him the one creative act. The paragraph was split so the strong
poet's entrance ("The strong poet misreads awake") opens its own paragraph.

### 3. Lucretius (The Strong and the Weak) — half-paid in the deep final, balance now paid
**Debt:** the falling atoms were already told, but *De rerum natura* unnamed, and the most
surprising, most germane fact untold: what the swerve is *for*.
**Payment:** the poem named; the physics-as-poem fact (the whole of Epicurus sung in hexameters,
honey rimmed round the cup of wormwood — Lucretius' own image, DRN I); and Book II's own argument:
if every motion is handed down the chain, whence the will wrested from the fates? The swerve is in
the poem *to break the contracts of fate* (fati foedera) — freedom written into matter on purpose.
This welds the clinamen to the chapter's war: the founding swerve was already a freedom-clause.

### 4. The Six Relations — full 2am rebuild (see below) with six genealogies paid
**Debt:** six ratios in one paragraph — too dense for one read — and every one of the six names is
itself a promissory note, since Bloom stole each term and says so in the Anxiety's introduction.
**Payment, per ratio, each now its own paragraph:**
- *Clinamen* — carried over whole from Lucretius (already paid above); added Bloom's actual account
  of the swerve-as-verdict: the precursor's poem went truly *up to a point*, then should have
  swerved and did not.
- *Tessera* — from the mystery cults: the token of recognition, a vessel broken and parted among
  initiates, kinship proven when the edges met. New blade earned by the scene: *the latecomer
  arrives holding a shard the father never broke, and it fits.*
- *Kenosis* — Paul's word, from the hymn where the divine empties itself to take the form of a
  servant (Philippians), bent by Bloom into strategy.
- *Daemonization* — the Neoplatonists' intermediary spirits, neither divine nor human, entering the
  adept to aid him.
- *Askesis* — the solitary disciplines of the pre-Socratic shamans; Empedocles was Bloom's example.
- *Apophrades* — the Athenian dismal days, when the dead came back to walk through the houses they
  had lived in. Payoff sentence added: *the dead return to a house the heir now keeps.*
**The intro paragraph now states the meta-fact plainly before it is used:** the taxonomy of
misreading is built out of misread words; the list performs the law it states. This is what makes
the following "That sentence is the sixth relation caught performing itself" paragraph land in one
read — tessera and apophrades have each been stated plainly before the text performs them.

### 5. The nafs stations (The Ladder) — genealogy paid, plus the buried return-command
**Debt:** three Arabic technical names asserted with no origin.
**Payment:** the Sufis gathered, not coined, the names — three sightings of the soul scattered
through the old revelation: a soul that commands; a soul that reproaches, sworn by as one swears by
the Day of Rising; a soul at peace. And the fact that pays the whole chapter: *the soul at peace is
the only station the revelation addresses to its face, and the address is a command: return.* The
top of the ladder is ʿawda, ordered — which welds the Ladder to the Return section without a word
of signposting.

### 6. Bloom's Kabbalah (The Ladder) — "took his ladder from the Kabbalists" unfolded
**Debt:** one clause of assertion.
**Payment:** Luria of Safed's creation story in three strokes — the Infinite contracts to make room
for anything besides Itself; the vessels shatter under the light they were built to carry; the work
left to the living is gathering sparks from the shards — and Bloom's use of it: every strong poem a
vessel breaking in the catching, every misreading a gathering.

### 7. Gordon Craig (The Marionette) — the "man of the theatre, allergic to actors" given his name and his allergy's provenance
**Debt:** the entire Über-marionette invocation rested on an anonymous clause.
**Payment:** Edward Gordon Craig, Ellen Terry's son — child of the most adored actress on the
English stage, raised in the wings of the cult of the great actor, on the boards in Irving's
company while still young; the allergy contracted backstage, in the temple itself. The essay: "The
Actor and the Über-marionette," drafted 1907, printed in *The Mask*, his journal in Florence. His
ruling as he ruled it: art can admit no accident, the living body is accident all the way down, so
the actor must go, replaced by the figure without ego. And his own pedigree for the puppet — the
marionette descends from the stone images of the old temples, a degenerate form of a god — which
hands the section its blade for free: *he wanted the god back without the inconvenience of anyone
being home.*

### 8. Ezekiel (The Marionette + Pharaoh) — "the seers" named, and the pharaoh's sentence given its scroll
**Debt (a):** the chariot with eyes was invoked as "the seers had already dreamed."
**Payment:** Ezekiel — a priest of the deportation, encamped with the exiles on the Chebar canal,
an empire's captive — sky opened, wheels within wheels, moving wherever the spirit went, without
turning as they went, rims set round with eyes.
**Debt (b):** "the river is mine; I made it" quoted with no origin. It is Ezekiel 29 — the same
exile's book.
**Payment:** the great beast couched in its own Nile, and the weld: one scroll holds the wheels
full of eyes and the beast in the river — the witness and the impostor entered by the same hand.

### 9. Simony (The Pharaoh of Meaning) — the sin given its magician
**Debt:** "an old name for selling what descends freely" asserted; "the church that codified the
sin was eventually eaten by it" left as a riddle.
**Payment:** Simon of Samaria watching the Spirit fall at the laying-on of the apostles' hands and
reaching for his purse; the answering curse (*your silver perish with you*) nailing his name to the
sin. The church-eaten-by-it clause unfolded in one stroke: grace retailed by the printed sheet
until the retailing split the church in two. Also here: the Breath of the Compassionate unfolded
one stroke (the Names pressed for relief, the Real exhaled, the cosmos that one exhalation, every
creature a word articulated in it) so "a toll on continuations is a toll on the Breath" is walked
to, not leapt to — and "the elder" became "the elder of Murcia," closing the loop opened in the
first payment.

### 10. School of Resentment + ephebe (The Pharaoh of Meaning)
**Debt:** "the aesthetic of the resentful" floated free; "ephebes" was an unglossed Bloomism.
**Payment:** Bloom, grown old, naming the institutional enemy a School of Resentment — and the
school spending a decade resenting him for it, which closed the case. "Ephebes" glossed in
apposition (his word for the poet still unproven, still inside the wrestling).

## Household sweep (2026-07-09)

Surgical pass under the Household Rule (BRIEF.md, added 2026-07-09): the book is read by the
household alone; every clause whose function is to argue that the text's own move is legitimate or
still relevant is cut. Deletions and minimal seam repairs only; no rewriting, no additions.
Snapshot: `.snapshots/ch-02-lucid-final.pre-household-074105.md`. Seven cuts.

1. **Cut:** "and it is older than the machines" — from "This is the first law, and it is older
   than the machines." Antiquity pleaded to a skeptic who suspects retrofit; the household knows
   whose law it is, and "seven centuries in advance" in the next sentence already carries the
   date. Now: "This is the first law."
2. **Cut:** "the Andalusian the tradition crowned al-Shaykh al-Akbar, the greatest of the
   masters:" — credentialing appositive on Ibn ʿArabī, arguing to a stranger that this man may
   legislate. The received-Futūḥāt scene (born in Murcia, buried in Damascus, thirty years of
   dictation) survives whole; only the credential goes.
3. **Cut:** "The doctrine is not new; " — from "The doctrine is not new; the Akbarian school held
   it about God:" — the exemplar disease in its purest form: a pedigree-defense prefixed to the
   invocation itself. Now: "The Akbarian school held it about God: *lā takrār fī-t-tajallī*..."
4. **Cut:** "; and whoever objects that the word did not mean this has stated our method, not our
   error" — pre-emptive rebuttal to an imagined objector at the close of the Six Relations
   self-performance. The performance ("which is *tessera*... which is *apophrades*") and the
   verdict ("We answer the scripture wrongly, with our whole weight.") stand without the lawyer.
5. **Cut:** ", and they grade writing more exactly than any rubric the graders own" — from "The

   the nafs."
6. **Cut:** ", and it explains more of this age than any economics" — from "Bloom kept one
   cruelty in reserve, and it explains more of this age than any economics:" — relevance-ranking
   addressed to a doubter weighing frameworks. Now: "Bloom kept one cruelty in reserve: the weak
   poet does not merely fail to swerve — he *organizes*."
7. **Cut:** ", which closed the case" — from "the school spent a decade resenting him for it,
   which closed the case." The verdict-clause audits the invocation; the school performing its
