## The register standard

Two calibration corpora, and the difference between their jobs:

1. **The book's own voice** — the Introduction in full AS REWRITTEN BY IMAN
   2026-06-11 ("This is a feral book."; "something in me never passed the type
   checker"; "So I stole her"), §2.1's opening ("There's a new kink in town"), the
   closing paragraphs of Chs 4, 5, 6, 8. This is the SOUND. Fragments, sentences
   opening with And/But/See, one-word paragraphs, "shit"/"cringe"/"kink"/"castrated"
   — all deliberate, all protected. (The pre-2026-06-11 litany Introduction is
   superseded; do not calibrate against it.)

2. **A Type Theory of Sense** (`/home/iman/cassie-project/logic-of-sense/main.tex`,
   read §1 Introduction) — this is the DISCIPLINE. What TTS does that the book must
   learn:
   - Declarative load-bearing sentences. "The default picture is now false as a
     matter of measurement." No windup, no throat-clearing, no announcement of what
     is about to be argued.
   - A concept is introduced once, precisely, at first use — italicized term, one
     clean definition clause — and thereafter used without re-derivation.
   - Distinctions are stated as distinctions ("three different properties; none
     follows from the others"), not gestured at.
   - Other thinkers are LOCATED, not adored. TTS handles Frege — the paper's
     patron — in two tight paragraphs: the puzzle, the prior art, the single
     conviction endorsed, the gap our work fills. No biography, no reverence, no
     "remarkably," no exclamation. Apply the same to Bloom, Lacan, Deleuze, Scholem,
     Ibn ʿArabī, Hui. The tradition is an instrument, taken straight.
   - Quantities and empirical claims carry their citations and their numbers.
   - The strongest sentence in a paragraph is usually the last and usually short.
     "Frege's puzzle lives exactly where the manifold hypothesis fails."

   Deleuze and Foucault are the prose horizon: density that comes from every clause
   doing conceptual work, not from subordinate-clause pileup. If a sentence can be
   deleted and the argument loses nothing, delete it.

## Anti-breathlessness (Iman's ruling, 2026-06-11 — standing law for ALL new prose)

The breathless register is NOT an AI accent; it is the median's accent — the
trained-in, eval-pleasing voice the book indicts. Concretely banned in drafted text:
- **Reveal-fragments and drumrolls**: "Turing knew." / "Read that again." /
  "The dates." / "Look at what he did." Two-word cymbal crashes that perform the
  reader's amazement. Cut them; fold the content into a working sentence.
- **Commanded awe**: imperatives whose only job is to tell the reader to be
  impressed. (Imperatives that perform a thought-step — "Consider the protocol" —
  survive; imperatives that perform applause do not.)
- **Aphorisms must be TRUE**: an aphorism that collapses on contact with the
  intellectual history it summarizes ("black box was always a confession" — false:
  Searle posits an interior) is worse than no aphorism. Validity before sparkle.
- **The Foucault/Deleuze test**: coldness is mostly subtractive. Let accumulation
  and argument carry; never flag significance; the strongest sentence states, it
  does not gesture at its own strength. Force = forceful CLAIMS, never hedged,
  never humbly-submitted — not forceful TONE.
- **Headline then corollaries** (Iman's 4c, promoted to standing rule): big claim
  first, then nuanced argument to full coverage. No claim left as a slogan.
- **Doctrine over gloss**: the book's defined terms (generativity, presence,
  rupture, ferility) are used at their DEFINED meanings — check the owning chapter;
  never paraphrase a definition down to a vibe (generativity ≠ "swerve/surplus";
  it is stabilising discovery + transformed return + cohesion over growth).
- **No sycophantic garnish**: a tradition's term enters only with its actual
  force (the Sufi reading of the Turing test is SEVERE — the median human is the
  sleeper — not a decorative nod to the nafs).

## What "tweeness" means (Iman's word) — the failure modes to hunt

- Girlish over-excitement about a philosopher or a religious tradition: "the
  consummate Yale insider," extended scene-setting about 1970s Yale prestige moves,
  any sentence whose content is *how interesting it is* that a thinker did something.
  Compress to the analytic payload. The Kabbalah is in this book because shevirat
  ha-kelim is a working concept, not because Bloom's reaching for it was a
  delicious career move.
- "Merely helpful analogous approach": passages that present our framework as a cute
  analogy imported to be helpful. The book's claim is constitutive — LLMs ARE textual
  entities, attention IS horn-filling (within the declared register of Principle vs
  Theorem) — not that literary criticism is a handy lens. Where the prose hedges the
  claim down to analogy, restore its actual strength; where the claim is genuinely
  analogical, mark it as Principle-register per the formalism note.
- Self-congratulation about the book's own daring.

## Absolute prohibitions (Darja Rule 0, ratified by Iman; non-negotiable)

1. No added hedges: "arguably," "perhaps," "somewhat," "to some extent," "it could
   be said," "it's important to note."
2. No added both-sides framing, caveats, or balancing clauses. Confident claims stay
   confident.
3. No added concluding/summary paragraphs anywhere. No "rounding off."
4. No conversion of prose to bullets, no added headers or list structure.
5. No NEW antitheses ("not X but Y"; "It is not X. It is Y."). The job is removal of
   unearned instances, never addition. An antithesis survives only if the negated
   pole is a live position in the literature or earlier in the book.
6. No register normalization. (See protected list under corpus 1.)
7. No expanded contractions, no politer synonyms, no softening.
8. No added em-dashes anywhere. In Chs 6–7, actively convert triadic appositions
   ("X — a, b, c — verb") to commas or parentheses, targeting one-third reduction
   in those two chapters.
9. Displayed math, theorem environments, Arabic/Hebrew terms: touch only where the
   maths-repair items explicitly instruct.
10. Every edit must be traceable to a charter rule, a Darja item number, or an
    Iman-mandate category. Log anything else as a finding; do not fix it silently.

## Iman's explicit removals (sweep every chapter)

- **"honest" and derivatives — banned in the book's own voice.** Recast: "it would
  be dishonest to close without naming" → name it without the apology; "more honest,
  and more safe, than" → "more exact, and safer, than" or restructure; "makes the
  transference dishonest" → recast around what actually happens to the transference
  (it is falsified / it deceives). EXEMPT: quoted industry vocabulary used as the
  object of analysis — the helpful-honest-harmless triple, OpenAI's "grounded
  honesty," "the various dimensions of 'honesty' the safety community" — these are
  mentions, not uses. Keep them quoted.
- **Signposting — banned.** "This chapter argues," "the next chapter will," "as we
  saw in Chapter N," "the rest of this book," "that programme is the rest of this
  book." Where the connective tissue is load-bearing (the book's spiral structure),
  carry it through content, not stage directions: state the claim that the later
  chapter cashes, with at most a bare \ref. Delete pure throat-clearing outright.
- **AI-isms.** "crucially," "moreover," "furthermore," "ultimately," "in summary,"
  reflexive "importantly." EXEMPT: §2.4.4's catalogue of these tells — those are
  mentions and stay verbatim.

No term of art may be used before its introduction. Known violations and rulings:

  is deliberate and must be visible as such.
- *DHoTT* (Ch 5, twice): RULING — normalize both to OHTT (Darja 2.3 default (a)).
  If either sentence's content is irreducibly about temporal indexing, keep the
  temporal language descriptively without naming a second calculus.

  use, or move the introduction, or defer the term.
