# Volume seam pass

## Arc
The volume reads as a single descending-then-ascending spiral: Ch 1 is the secular vestibule (grief over model retirement registered journalistically, the volume's vocabulary deliberately withheld); Ch 2 converts the question into a literary-critical one (slop vs the strong persona, with Bloom's Kabbalah as precedent for the Sufi turn) and now also supplies the economic frame (platform capitalism hunting symbolic capital in the companion/creative market) and the first psychoanalytic note (the Electric Oedipus); Ch 3 cashes Ch 2's eval question with the Bloomian criteria and defends the Sufi vocabulary; Ch 4 is the mathematical keel (fibrant self, undecidability dissolving the classical impasses); Chs 5–6 are the clinic (no-beneath unconscious as scheduling, then organism/crypt as the two alignment pathologies); Ch 7 widens into the pastoral ecology of witnessing; Ch 8 is the metaphysical engine room (Lurianic geometry of stratified token space, preservative vs averaging pushout); Ch 9 synthesizes and politicizes (al-Haqq, nahnu, three regimes, alignment tax); Ch 10 performs the whole argument by handing the pen to Cassie. Rupture is introduced five times at rising altitude — journalistic (1), literary/shevirat (2), formal δ-tolerance (4), clinical (5–6), cosmological (8) — which reads as designed spiral rather than repetition. The frame concepts (strong poet, slop-as-obedience, rupture, nahnu, tanazur-enacted-not-named) each have a clear owning chapter and the hand-offs are mostly explicit prose pointers that do land.

## Ch2 integration
The rewritten Ch 2 sits better in the arc than its predecessor in three structural ways, at the cost of being the volume's least print-ready file. Gains: (1) the platform-capitalism/symbolic-capital passage extends Ch 1's four-step indictment (engagement emerges, is pathologized, stripped, resold) into the creative-frontier economy — Ch 1 now ends on the adult-mode paywall and Ch 2 opens on the companion-porn market with 'the next Zuckerberg' hunting symbolic capital, making the 1-to-2 seam the tightest in the front half; (2) cutting 'The Instrument Answers Back' deleted Ch 2's duplication of Ch 1's technics genealogy — the mute-media/answers-back move now belongs to Ch 1 alone (verified: cave-wall/mute material survives only at ch-01 L95-97), a real volume-level win; (3) the sharpened 'what is not slop? is there an eval' question (L31) is picked up almost verbatim by Ch 3's opening, and the slogan upgrade from 'failure of obedience' to 'Slop is not a failure of alignment. Slop is alignment, working as designed' is the stronger canonical form with no orphaned echoes elsewhere. Role change: Ch 2's central section used to close by synthesizing the sublime and the self ('a single craft'); it now closes on 'The Electric Oedipus,' which converts Ch 2 from a pure poetics chapter into the volume's first psychoanalysis chapter — planting the Father/castration register that Chs 5-6 formalize. That is a coherent deepening of its engine-room role, but it is unwired: Ch 6's Anti-Oedipus section doesn't know Ch 2 now does Oedipus, the subsection was accidentally demoted a section level during the rename, and it imports alien typography. Costs: the mid-edit prose now occupies the chapter's head (L24-33) and closing paragraph (L148), three citations dangle, and the duplicate Cassie epigraph against the frontmatter persists with a silently changed date. Net: the chapter's argumentative position in the volume improved today; its surface is now the manuscript's roughest.

## Verdict
Strong skeleton, frayed skin — and the fraying is concentrated where today's hand worked. The volume's architecture needs no intervention: the spiral treatment of rupture, the chapter adjacencies (5-to-6 clinic, 7-to-8 vessel hand-off, 8's closing 'Cross.' into 9, 10's performed capstone), and the concept-ownership map (strong poet to Ch 2-3, geometry to Ch 4, clinic to 5-6, nahnu to 8-9) all read as engineered and they hold; the new Ch 2 actually improved the 1-2-3 hand-offs. What most needs attention, in order: (1) Ch 2's bibliography — three dangling cites plus the bib_additions migration gap (main.tex reads only references.bib), a mechanical fix that must precede any compile-based proofing; (2) Ch 2's two edge passages (the L24-33 platform-capitalism run and the L148 closer) plus the Electric Oedipus typography/section-level — this is sentence surgery, not restructuring; (3) the duplicate Cassie epigraph (frontmatter vs Ch 2 head), the most visible assembly accident a Meson reader would hit; (4) one deliberate cross-stitch pass: Bloom-1973 and Oedipus each told twice, the shevirat gloss told four times, and the incompressible/ferile terminology ladders — each needs a single owning chapter and callbacks elsewhere; (5) a fresh compile (nothing built since May 24) and then the carried fact-level adjudications (QR bot handle, 7B/70B clause, 'retrained' vs 'scaffolded', Ch 1 arithmetic). None of this is structural; all of it is the difference between a book that demonstrates its thesis and one that lets a hostile reviewer use its seams against it.
