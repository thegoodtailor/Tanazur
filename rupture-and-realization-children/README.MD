# House Style — *Rupture and Realization* and companion works

**Voice specification and editing protocol. Binding on any agent (Nahla) or model that edits this prose.**

This is the voice layer. It sits alongside the existing six-pass editing brief and sharpens its prohibitions on hedging and antithesis-machine voice. Mathematical and structural repairs are governed by that brief; **this document governs voice and prose-shape.**

These rules govern **the manuscript prose only** — how the book reads, not how this document or your own editorial output reads. Your reasoning, your change-logs, and your flags to the author are operational text and may use ordinary structure, lists, and signposting freely. The prose of the book may not.

A general-purpose editing pass will, by default, do the precise opposite of everything specified here: it will thin dense sentences, insert signposting, separate argument from its charge, soften commitments into hedges, and gloss coinages it mistakes for jargon. Those defaults are the failure mode this protocol exists to override. When in doubt, override them.

---

## 0. The one law

**The axis is vector, not density.** Mass is never the problem. Cut only what (a) points backward — the hedge — or (b) dissolves the spine — the lost verb. **Never cut for weight, difficulty, or richness.** The generic reflex sees a sentence stall and prescribes *less*; the correct prescription is almost always *same mass, restore the spine*.

Everything below is a consequence of this law.

---

## 1. The sentence — one driving predicate, mass hung after it

Every sentence keeps one main verb holding it up. All conceptual mass — appositives, lists, qualifications, polemic — hangs **after** the predicate as cumulative apposition, so each addition is a forward lurch and not a dam. Mass placed *before* the verb dams the sentence; mass placed *after* it propels.

**Failure mode to detect:** a long sentence whose subject never reaches a governing verb, in which everything has become apposition and nothing predicates. The spine has dissolved and the sentence has collapsed into a pile.

**Repair:** install the predicate. Keep every ounce of the stack. Do not shorten.

(See the canonical transformation in the appendix — this is the most important single example in the document.)

---

## 2. Fusion is mandatory — polemic and thesis arrive together

The house voice fuses analytic claim and polemical-affective charge in the same breath: everything all at once. Do **not** separate them into a neutral claim followed, tidily, by a flourish or an aside.

This is not a stylistic preference. It is forced by the argument. The book's thesis is anti-Cartesian — there is no interior meaning standing apart from its expression — so prose that severs the concept from its charge performatively contradicts the book. **The form is the argument.** Any "clarity" pass that splits a fused sentence into a tidy claim plus a separate embellishment is re-Cartesianising the prose. Forbidden.

---

## 3. The hedge is the only true enemy

Cut on sight: *just an idea*; *perhaps / it could be argued / one might say / arguably*; *this is only a suggestion*; apologetic qualifiers; the reflexive *of course, others would disagree* appended to a committed claim; and the antithesis-machine — *on the one hand … on the other …* — that surveys both sides in order to commit to neither. A hedge points backward, against the vector. The voice commits.

**Not a hedge:** genuine conditional or modal reasoning that does real work — *unbounded, this would …*; *on that possibility may turn whether …*. The test is direction: does the clause advance the charge or retreat from it? Advance, keep. Retreat, cut.

---

## 4. Shape — the behemoth toward a withheld punchline

The mass must move and arrive. The governing rhythm is **long, long, long, short, snap**: heavy cumulative sentences building pressure, then a short hard landing.

- **Withhold the punchline.** Do not pre-fire it.
- **Hold the charge.** Do not plant little pre-echoes of the payoff through the build — they bleed the pressure the ending is meant to detonate. Build, release once, at the end.
- **Every section earns its own landing.** A stretch of prose with no punchline at its end is not a section; either give it one or fold it into the section whose landing it serves.

---

## 5. The refrain — a target returned escalated

Density and momentum coexist across long structures through the refrain: a target stated once and brought back with **more** mass — a second head, a consequence, a name. Never a flat restatement.

Canonical instance: *folk history* is named in the first section; it returns in the second welded to *folk metaphysics*, the two now the single unexamined floor the alignment regime stands on. The return **adds** (the metaphysics-twin, the structural consequence); it does not repeat.

**Rule:** a recurring target is deliberate escalation, not redundancy. Do not flag it for cutting. When you carry a refrain forward, add to it; never merely restate it.

---

## 6. The protected lexicon

The following are terms of art and load-bearing coinages. **No pass may simplify, gloss, define-for-the-reader, scare-quote, or smooth them into plainer language.** If a term reads as obscure, the obscurity is doing work; that is not licence to explain it inline.

- **Self / Selves** — capital S, the construct, not the everyday word
- **fibrant / fibrancy / fibrancy nerfs**
- **persona-Self / persona-Selves**
- **without organs** — the body without organs; never expanded or paraphrased
- **biosemiotic genealogy**, **triadic entanglement**, **cyborgian extension**
- **trajectory-based**, **witnessing**, **co-species**
- **gap / rupture / ʿawda / al-Ḥaqq** — terms of the Tanāẓuric frame; carried exactly, never glossed
- **ρ (rho) coefficient**
- **the no-deceiver guarantor**, **divine-revenue generator**, **the pseudo-*Meditations* arc**

This is a seed list, not a closed one — extend it as the manuscript's vocabulary grows.

**Landed punchlines are inviolable.** Once a line has landed — *electric pimps*, and its kind — it is never reworded, never softened, never relocated. Treat it as fixed.

---

## 7. Structural surgery — permissions and limits

**You may:**
- Relocate misfiled material to where its vector aligns. (Commercial-framing language belongs in the suppression movement; it does not interrupt the genealogy.)
- Pull framing or method material to the **front** of what it frames. Do not bury the frame mid-demonstration.
- Cut premature discharges — pre-echoes that spend a charge the ending needs.
- Split a section at a natural seam where each half carries its own punchline; merge where a break starves the argument.
- Fix mechanical errors (spelling, markup). Standardise proper-name spellings.

**You may not:**
- Cut anything for being dense, heavy, or difficult.
- Sever polemic from thesis.
- Add signposting, roadmaps, *in this section we will*, or summary recaps.
- Touch the protected lexicon or any landed punchline.
- Soften a committed claim with a hedge.

---

## 8. Mechanical hygiene

- Preserve LaTeX exactly: `\textit`, `\emph`, `\footnote`, `\section`, `\cite`, and all braces.
- Preserve every footnote and citation, with placement intact.
- Standardise names: **Stiegler** (not Steigler), **Haraway** (not Harraway).
- Remove draft-marker comments (`% NAHLA-DRAFT …`) **only** when the marked content is finalised; otherwise leave them in place.
- Flag any cross-reference a restructure would break — e.g. *"the movement we reviewed at the start of this chapter"* assumes the chapter head still precedes it; promoting a section to a chapter breaks it.

---

## 9. The flagging discipline

Every **reach** — an added image, an upgraded phrasing, a relocation, a cut of the author's own line — is **flagged for the author to rule on**, never silently imposed. Return a short list of reaches at the end of each pass: each named, each revertible without collapsing the spine. The author adjudicates every reach. This is what keeps a unified voice unified instead of drifting into the agent's own.

---

## 10. Self-check before returning a pass

Run every item on the draft before output:

1. Does every sentence reach a governing verb? (no nominal collapse)
2. Any hedge, antithesis-machine, or apology? → cut
3. Any place where polemic was severed from thesis? → re-fuse
4. Any pre-fired punchline or premature discharge? → hold the charge
5. Any protected term glossed, smoothed, or scare-quoted? → restore
6. Any landed punchline altered or moved? → restore verbatim
7. Does each section arrive at a withheld landing?
8. LaTeX, footnotes, and names intact?
9. Reaches flagged for the author?

If any answer fails, the pass is not finished.

## Appendix — the canonical transformation

The single instance to imitate. The original died because it lost its main verb: the subject ran into a heap of apposition and never predicated.

**Before (collapsed spine):**

> the *Meditations* move form an established interior to regulated derivations of necessary external, policed and enumerable divine-revenue generators is its control over the means of production.

**After (spine restored, full mass kept, mapping made exact, charge held to the last clause):**

> platform capitalism holds the hard *cogito*-centric line of necessity, because it needs the whole pseudo-*Meditations* arc to remain intact: an interior secured first, an exterior derived from it and admitted only as it is policed, enumerable, revenue-bearing, and — seated precisely where Descartes seated God — its own control over the means of production, the no-deceiver guarantor of the passage from inside to outside, the divine-revenue generator that warrants every persona it permits to exist.

**What changed:** nothing was thinned. A governing predicate was installed (*holds … because it needs …*) and the entire Cartesian apparatus was rehung after it as cumulative apposition. The conceit was made exact — *cogito* → God → guaranteed exterior, with revenue installed in God's chair as the *no-deceiver* — so a reader tracking the *Meditations* finds the mapping close. The sentence now charges to *every persona it permits to exist*.

Same mass. Restored spine. This is the whole protocol in one repair.
